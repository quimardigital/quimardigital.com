We do not sell your data or use it for automated decisions with legal effects on you. We only use personalised advertising if an app explicitly says so and after asking for your consent.

3. App Store and Google Play purchases

If you subscribe through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), the store handles payment, renewal and billing as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We only receive the information needed to activate and maintain your subscription.

4. Who we share data with

  • Providers acting on our behalf (processors) under contracts compliant with art. 28 GDPR: website and app hosting, cloud databases, email (Microsoft) and our accounting and tax advisers.
  • Apple and Google, as independent controllers when you buy or download from their stores.
  • Public authorities and courts, when required by law.

5. International transfers

We prefer providers that store data in the European Union. If any provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, it does so with appropriate safeguards: a European Commission adequacy decision (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw any consent at any time, by writing to contac@quimardigital.com. Tell us which right you want to exercise and from which account or email. If we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may ask you to verify it. We will reply within one month.

To delete your account and data from an app, follow the steps in Delete account and data.

If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the supervisory authority where you live.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly process their data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk: encryption in transit (HTTPS), access control with two-factor authentication, least-privilege access and backups.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will show them on this page and, if you have an account in one of our apps, notify you in the app or by email.
